# User Feedback Supporting Flow Simplifications for Merchant Onboarding

## Submission Portal Experience

- "The submission portal feels overwhelming and confusing; I often don't know what documents are needed or where to upload them."
- "I wish there were tooltips or help icons that explained each step clearly. It would save me a lot of time and frustration."

## Automated Verification Process

- "I have no idea what’s happening after I submit my documents. It would be great to see real-time updates on the verification status."
- "The process feels like a black box. More transparency would help me trust that my application is being reviewed properly."

## Manual Review Queue

- "I got no notification when my submission was delayed in the review queue. Waiting without updates makes me anxious."
- "It felt like my application got lost because I wasn’t informed of the delays or next steps."

## Email Notifications

- "The emails I receive are unclear and don’t tell me what to do next. I end up having to contact support for clarification."
- "Timely and clear notifications would improve my experience and help me move forward without confusion."

## Overall User Journey

- "The whole onboarding feels disjointed. It’s hard to know what’s next and how far along I am in the process."
- "I would really appreciate a feedback option so I can share my experience and help improve the process for others."

---

These user quotes highlight common frustrations and needs experienced during merchant onboarding, underscoring the importance of proposed flow simplifications and improved communication.
