# Flow Simplifications: Detailed Insights and User Quotes

## 1. Submission Portal Experience
- Users feel the portal is overwhelming and confusing.
- They often don't know what documents are needed or where to upload them.
- User Quotes:
  - "The submission portal feels overwhelming and confusing; I often don't know what documents are needed or where to upload them."
  - Users request tooltips or help icons to explain each step clearly.

## 2. Automated Verification Process
- Users feel uncertain about what happens after submitting documents.
- They want real-time updates on verification status.
- User Quotes:
  - "I have no idea what’s happening after I submit my documents. It would be great to see real-time updates on the verification status."
  - Users want more transparency to trust the review process.

## 3. Manual Review Queue
- Users experience anxiety due to lack of notifications about delays.
- They feel their applications get lost without updates.
- User Quotes:
  - "I got no notification when my submission was delayed in the review queue. Waiting without updates makes me anxious."
  - "It felt like my application got lost because I wasn’t informed of the delays or next steps."

## 4. Email Notifications
- Users find email notifications unclear and unhelpful.
- They often need to contact support for clarification.
- User Quotes:
  - "The emails I receive are unclear and don’t tell me what to do next. I end up having to contact support for clarification."
  - Timely and clear notifications would improve the experience.

## 5. Overall User Journey
- Users find the onboarding process disjointed and confusing.
- They want to know the next steps and their progress.
- User Quotes:
  - "The whole onboarding feels disjointed. It’s hard to know what’s next and how far along I am in the process."
  - Users appreciate having a feedback option to share their experience.

---

This document compiles key user feedback and pain points to guide ongoing flow simplifications and improve the user experience in onboarding processes.
