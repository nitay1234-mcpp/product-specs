# Merchant Onboarding Process Improvements: Accessibility and User Experience Summary

## Main Goals
- Reduce merchant onboarding time to under 24 hours through automation.
- Enhance accessibility to ensure inclusivity for users with disabilities.
- Simplify submission portal and improve communication to reduce user confusion and anxiety.
- Increase transparency and real-time feedback during the verification process.
- Establish continuous user feedback channels for ongoing improvement.

## Key Design Enhancements

### Accessibility Improvements
- Portal interface enhancements to support screen readers, keyboard navigation, and cognitive impairments.
- Email notifications formatted for accessibility and clarity.
- Clear, contextual help and FAQs tailored to diverse user needs.
- Comprehensive user journey analysis focused on accessibility barriers.
- Compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

### User Experience Enhancements
- Intuitive and user-friendly submission portal with grouped document uploads, tooltips, inline validation, and progress indicators.
- Real-time status tracking and transparent communication of automated and manual review stages.
- Proactive notifications about manual review delays and next steps.
- Redesigned email templates for clear, actionable communication.
- End-to-end journey mapping with visual progress indicators.
- Feedback mechanisms integrated within the onboarding process.
- Usability testing and iterative improvements based on user feedback.

## Relevant Specifications
- **Merchant Onboarding v2:** Focus on automation, accessibility, feedback, and monitoring metrics.
- **Merchant Onboarding Feature Spec:** Online submission, automated KYB verification, manual review queue, and email notifications.
- **Submission Portal Experience Spec:** UI redesign, contextual help, validation, and FAQ inclusion.
- **User Experience Improvement Issues:** Identifies pain points and recommends enhancements based on research.

## Planned Actions
- Conduct accessibility audits and implement portal redesigns with expert collaboration.
- Develop and integrate automated verification with AI and third-party KYB services.
- Implement real-time status updates and notification systems.
- Redesign email notifications to be clear, accessible, and timely.
- Launch feedback tools and monitor user experience metrics.
- Conduct usability testing and iterate designs based on findings.
- Ensure compliance with security, privacy, and accessibility regulations.

This summary encapsulates the strategic direction and tactical plans to improve the merchant onboarding process, emphasizing accessibility and user experience enhancements for higher satisfaction and operational efficiency.
