# Merchant Onboarding User Feedback Improvement Plan

## 1. Introduction
This document outlines a comprehensive plan to address key issues identified through user feedback in the merchant onboarding process. The goal is to simplify the submission experience, increase transparency, improve communication, and enhance the overall onboarding journey for merchants.

## 2. Identified Issues from User Feedback
- Submission portal feels overwhelming and confusing.
- Lack of clarity on required documents and upload steps.
- Opaque verification process with no real-time updates.
- Anxiety caused by uncommunicated delays in manual review queue.
- Unclear and unhelpful email notifications.
- Disjointed onboarding journey with no clear progress indicators.
- Lack of feedback options for merchants to share their experience.

## 3. Plan for Improvement

### 3.1 Simplify Submission Portal
- Redesign the UI for clarity and ease of use.
- Provide a clear checklist of required documents upfront.
- Add tooltips, help icons, and inline instructions.
- Conduct usability testing with merchants.

### 3.2 Increase Transparency in Verification Process
- Implement real-time status updates on document verification.
- Clearly explain each verification step and timelines.
- Notify merchants promptly of manual review requirements and potential delays.

### 3.3 Improve Communication and Notifications
- Redesign email notifications to be clear, actionable, and timely.
- Include next steps and required actions in all communications.
- Add progress indicators within emails and the portal.
- Provide easy access to support contacts.

### 3.4 Enhance Overall User Journey
- Introduce visible progress tracking throughout onboarding.
- Launch a feedback mechanism for merchants.
- Monitor support inquiries and feedback for ongoing improvements.

## 4. Timeline
- Months 1-2: Redesign submission portal and develop real-time status updates.
- Months 3-4: Revamp notification system and email templates; add progress indicators.
- Months 5-6: Launch feedback tools; conduct usability testing; gather feedback; iterate.

## 5. Expected Outcomes
- Reduced confusion and anxiety during submission.
- Increased transparency and trust throughout verification.
- Clear, actionable communications reducing support requests.
- A smoother and more satisfying onboarding experience.

---

This plan is designed to directly address merchant pain points identified in user feedback and foster a better onboarding experience through targeted improvements and continuous iteration.
