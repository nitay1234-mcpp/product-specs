# Proposal: Enhancing Merchant Onboarding Experience Through Flow Simplifications and Improved Communication

## Background
Recent user feedback on the merchant onboarding process has highlighted several pain points causing confusion, anxiety, and dissatisfaction. These insights reveal critical areas for improvement to streamline the onboarding journey, increase transparency, and enhance communication.

## Objectives
- Simplify the merchant onboarding submission portal to reduce user confusion.
- Increase transparency and real-time visibility into the verification process.
- Improve communication around manual review delays and next steps.
- Redesign email notifications to be clear, timely, and actionable.
- Create a cohesive and guided overall onboarding journey.
- Implement a feedback mechanism to capture user experiences continuously.

## Proposed Improvements and Action Plan

### 1. Submission Portal Experience
- Redesign the submission portal UI to be more intuitive and less overwhelming.
- Add contextual help elements such as tooltips and step-by-step guidance.
- Provide a clear, upfront checklist of required documents and upload instructions.
- Conduct usability testing with merchants to validate portal improvements.

### 2. Automated Verification Process
- Develop and integrate real-time status updates reflecting verification progress.
- Offer clear explanations of verification stages and what users can expect.
- Create a tracking dashboard accessible to merchants to monitor application status.
- Implement push notifications or alerts for key milestones in the verification process.

### 3. Manual Review Queue
- Establish notification triggers for delays or when manual review is required.
- Communicate estimated wait times and expected next steps proactively.
- Provide support contact options and status check-ins during manual review.
- Monitor manual review queue metrics to identify bottlenecks and improve throughput.

### 4. Email Notifications
- Redesign email templates emphasizing clarity, actionable instructions, and next steps.
- Ensure emails are sent promptly aligned with process milestones and user actions.
- Include relevant support resources and contact information in all communications.
- Test email effectiveness through user feedback and engagement metrics.

### 5. Overall User Journey
- Map the end-to-end onboarding process with defined stages and milestones.
- Implement visible progress indicators within the portal and communications.
- Standardize messaging to maintain consistency and reduce confusion.
- Launch a feedback tool allowing merchants to share their onboarding experience and suggestions.
- Use feedback data to inform ongoing improvements and prioritize enhancements.

## Timeline and Milestones
- Month 1-2: Design and prototype submission portal improvements and real-time status updates.
- Month 3: Implement notification system for manual review queue and redesign email templates.
- Month 4: Launch feedback mechanism and progress indicators.
- Month 5-6: Conduct usability testing, gather feedback, and iterate on improvements.

## Expected Outcomes
- Reduced merchant confusion and support inquiries related to onboarding.
- Increased user trust and satisfaction through transparency and timely communication.
- Faster resolution of manual reviews and decreased anxiety for merchants.
- Higher completion rates and positive feedback on the onboarding process.

---

This proposal aims to address key user pain points and enhance the merchant onboarding experience holistically. Implementation of these improvements will foster a smoother, clearer, and more user-friendly onboarding flow, ultimately benefiting merchants and the organization alike.
