# Merchant Onboarding Improvement Plan: Simplifying Submission and Enhancing Communication

## 1. Introduction
- Summarize user feedback highlighting confusion in submission and communication gaps.

## 2. Goals
- Simplify the submission portal to reduce user confusion and anxiety.
- Improve transparency and timeliness of communication throughout onboarding.
- Enhance user trust and satisfaction.
- Reduce onboarding time to under 24 hours using automated KYB (Know Your Business) processes.
- Establish clear success metrics to track improvements.

## 3. Success Metrics
- Measure reduction in support requests related to onboarding by X% within 3 months.
- Achieve Y% user satisfaction score in onboarding surveys.
- Onboarding completion time reduced to under 24 hours for Z% of merchants.
- Increase in automated verification rate to target %.

## 4. Risk Assessment and Mitigation
- Identify potential delays in automation implementation; mitigate with phased rollout.
- Address possible merchant resistance to new UI with training and support materials.
- Monitor manual review queue to prevent bottlenecks.
- Ensure data privacy and security compliance.

## 5. Simplifying Submission Process
- Redesign submission portal UI for clarity and ease of use.
- Provide clear checklist of required documents upfront.
- Add contextual help such as tooltips and instructions.
- Conduct usability testing with merchants for validation.
- Allow document submissions via a portal.
- Ensure accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA) in design and implementation.

## 6. Improving Communication
- Implement real-time status updates on application verification.
- Notify users promptly of manual review requirements and delays.
- Redesign email notifications for clarity, actionability, and timeliness.
- Introduce progress indicators within the onboarding journey.
- Provide easy access to support contacts and help resources.
- Automate verification processes for prompt results in most cases.
- Implement a manual review queue for edge cases with email notifications on status.
- Expand communication channels to include SMS and in-app notifications.

## 7. Technology and Integration
- Utilize [specify tools/platforms] for automation and notifications.
- Integrate onboarding status with CRM and support systems for seamless updates.
- Ensure secure data handling and compliance with relevant regulations.

## 8. Roles and Responsibilities
- Assign project lead for overall plan execution.
- Designate owners for submission portal redesign, communication improvements, automation, and feedback mechanisms.
- Establish cross-functional team collaboration.

## 9. Feedback and Iteration
- Launch feedback mechanism to gather ongoing user input.
- Monitor support inquiries and feedback to identify pain points.
- Iterate improvements based on data and user suggestions.
- Plan for continuous monitoring and quarterly reviews post-implementation.

## 10. Timeline
- Month 1-2: Redesign submission portal and develop real-time status updates.
- Month 3: Implement notification system and email redesign.
- Month 4: Launch progress indicators and feedback tools.
- Month 5-6: Conduct usability testing, gather feedback, and iterate.
- Month 7+: Continuous improvement and monitoring.

## 11. Expected Outcomes
- Reduced confusion and support requests.
- Increased user trust and satisfaction.
- Faster issue resolution and smoother onboarding flow.
- Reduced onboarding time through automation and streamlined processes.
